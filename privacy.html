Privacy Policy

Latlas · Nattskift Games · last updated 16 September 2026

Who we are

Latlas is published by Oleg Nochka, an independent developer, under the name Nattskift Games ("Nattskift Games", "we", "us"). Oleg Nochka is the person responsible for the app and for this policy.

The short version

We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and nothing about you is ever sent to us. Latlas does need the internet, because the imagery it shows comes from Google, and that means Google sees those requests. That part is explained below rather than buried.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following locally, and nowhere else:

  • Your finished games — the score, the date, the time limit and each round's result
  • Your stats, which are worked out from those games
  • Your settings — chosen time limit and whether sound is on

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We never see any of it, and there is no copy anywhere else, so it cannot be restored if you lose it.

Google Maps and Street View

The panoramas and the world map come from Google. When a round loads, your device asks Google for that imagery directly, and Google receives what it needs to answer — including your device's IP address. We do not send Google anything about you, and nothing in that exchange passes through us or is stored by us.

That use is governed by the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where you are

Latlas never asks for your location, and does not use it. The game is about guessing where the picture is, not where you are.

Children

Latlas is suitable for children. Since it collects nothing, there is nothing to collect from a child either. Street View imagery is real photography of public places and is shown as Google provides it.

Changes

If this ever changes — if a future version collects anything at all, or adds advertising — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried.
