Latlas

Guess where you are · Nattskift Games

Dropped somewhere on Earth. Work out where.

Each round puts you on a street somewhere in the world with nothing to go on but the view around you. Read the road markings, the plants, the light, the language on a sign. Then open the map and drop a pin. The closer you land, the more you score.

How it plays

Five rounds to a game. You can look around freely, so the answer is always somewhere in the view if you know what to look for. Scoring falls away with distance: landing in the right city is worth nearly everything, the right continent is worth something, the wrong hemisphere is worth almost nothing.

Where the places come from

Every location is checked before it ever reaches a round. Latlas uses only official Street View coverage, never the amateur photo spheres that turn up at the same coordinates, because a blurry phone panorama makes an unfair round. There are 760 vetted places across 34 countries, and each one is pinned to an exact panorama so the view you get is the view that was checked.

What you get

  • 760 verified locations across 34 countries, on every continent but Antarctica
  • Play at your own pace, or against a clock at two minutes, one minute or thirty seconds
  • Scores and stats kept on your device — best game, closest guess ever, countries seen
  • Personal bests tracked separately for each time limit, so they stay comparable
  • No account, no sign-up, and nothing to subscribe to
