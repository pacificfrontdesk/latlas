Common questions

A round will not load

Latlas needs an internet connection, because the imagery comes from Google rather than from inside the app. If a round sits on a dark screen, check your connection first. If it keeps happening on a good connection, tell us and include roughly when it happened.

Can I play offline?

No. The locations are built into the app, but the pictures are not — every panorama is fetched when the round starts. There is no way around that, and no version of it that works on a plane.

The clock ran out before I guessed

Whatever pin is on the map when time runs out is submitted. If there is no pin, the round scores nothing. The clock does not start until the panorama has actually appeared, so a slow connection does not eat your thinking time, and it keeps running while the map is open.

I lost my stats

History is kept on your device only. Deleting the app, resetting the device, or moving to a new phone without a backup will clear it, and we cannot restore it — there is no account and no server holding a copy.

Why is my best score split up?

Bests are kept separately for each time limit. A score set with no clock is not the same achievement as one set under thirty seconds, so they are not compared against each other.

The place name showed for a moment

It should not. If you ever see the name of the place while a round is still being played, that is a bug worth reporting — tell us what you saw and roughly where the round turned out to be.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes, with the speaker button on the main screen. The game also respects the ring and silent switch, and it never interrupts music or a podcast you already have playing.

Why are some places so remote?

Locations are drawn at random from places with official Street View coverage, which means plenty of empty road. That is the game: a stretch of nothing still tells you which part of the world you are in, if you read it carefully.
